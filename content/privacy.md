**This is a prototype.** It collects nothing. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or tracking. Anything you type into a demo form is kept in your own browser's storage and never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Every person, business and event shown is fictional and labelled so.

**On the real site**, this page would say who is responsible for the site (the chapter), what personal information is published (officers' roles and names, and photographs only with consent), how to ask for a photo to be removed, and — only if a members area is switched on — what member data is held, where it is kept (in Nigeria), how members see, download and delete their own data, and how to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
