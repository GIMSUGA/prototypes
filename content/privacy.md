**Who we are.** This website belongs to the GIMSUGA Ebonyi State chapter. The chapter is responsible for the personal information described here.

**What the public site shows.** The names and roles of chapter officers, reports of chapter events and a record of the chapter's work. Photographs of people appear only with their agreement, and can be taken down on request.

**If you are a member.** Your profile holds only what you choose to add. You decide whether you appear in the member directory and how members can reach you. You can see, download or delete your details at any time from your profile. Member details are stored in Nigeria and are never shown to people who are not signed in, or to search engines.

**No tracking.** The site sets no advertising or analytics cookies.

**Questions or complaints.** Contact the chapter through the details on the Join page. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
