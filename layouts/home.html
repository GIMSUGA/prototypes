GIMSUGA Abakaliki chapter · For member feedback

What should the chapter website be?

The chapter executive is considering a website. Before deciding anything, it wants members' views. This takes three steps: read, try, then tell us.

  1. 1. Read
  2. 2. Try the options
  3. 3. Tell us

Step 1

Read: what this is

These are working demos, not the real site. Every person, business and event in them is made up. You can click anything; nothing you type leaves your phone or computer.

The same in every option

  • Joining stays offline, through introduction by an existing member.
  • No dues, payments, bank details or donation buttons.
  • No public WhatsApp group links.
  • Officers listed by role and name, without personal phone numbers.
  • Photos of members only with their consent.

What they cost to run each year

A — Lean
About ₦0–₦7,200
B — Members area
About ₦260,000–₦660,000
C — Community portal
As B, plus volunteer time

B and C need a server in Nigeria and a paid developer, and make the chapter responsible for members' personal data. Estimates only.

The bar at the top lets you switch the option (A, B, C), see the site as a visitor, member or officer, and see it for Abakaliki alone or with other GIMSUGA chapters.

Step 2

Try the options

Each option has a few short stories. Tap one and it opens the demo at the right place, in the right view.

  • Option A

    Lean

    A clear public site for the chapter. No member logins, no member data.

    • A member wants the date and venue of the next meeting.
    • A new IMSU graduate in Abakaliki asks how to join.
    • The secretary adds an event, and another officer approves it.
    Explore option A

  • Option B

    Members area

    A, plus a private area where members find each other and members' businesses.

    • A member needs an accountant and checks members' businesses first.
    • A member moving to Enugu looks for GIMSUGA members there (choose Enugu in "Search in").
    • A member decides what to show, and how others can reach them.
    Explore option B

  • Option C

    Community portal

    B, plus news from members, RSVPs and event photo galleries.

    • A member says they will attend the end-of-year get-together.
    • A member shares news with the chapter.
    • An officer sees what needs reviewing before it appears.
    Explore option C

If other GIMSUGA chapters join

Any option can run for Abakaliki alone, or grow to include other chapters without rebuilding. See GIMSUGA with its chapters, or search members across chapters.

Step 3

Tell us what you think

A short questionnaire for chapter members, about 5 minutes. You will be asked to sign in to Google so each member answers once, but your email address is not recorded.

The questionnaire link goes here once the chapter executive has approved the questions.
