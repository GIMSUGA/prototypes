How to join

If you graduated from Imo State University and live in Ebonyi State, you are welcome in the chapter. Membership of GIMSUGA comes through a chapter.

  1. Talk to a member you know. New members are introduced to the chapter by an existing member.
  2. Don't know a member yet? Contact the chapter below and an officer will put you in touch.
  3. The executive takes it from there. They explain what membership involves and welcome you into the chapter.

Contact the chapter

These reach the chapter's own number and email, looked after by a chapter office.

The chapter never asks for dues or payment through this website. If anyone sends you a payment link claiming to be from the chapter, check with the executive first.
