Abakaliki chapter

How to join

If you graduated from Imo State University and live in or around Abakaliki, you are welcome in the chapter. Membership of GIMSUGA comes through a chapter.

  1. Talk to a member you know. New members are introduced to the chapter by an existing member.
  2. Don't know a member yet? Contact the chapter below, and an officer will put you in touch.
  3. The executive takes it from there. They explain what membership involves and add you to the chapter's own channels.

Contact the chapter

These reach the chapter's own number and email, looked after by a chapter office. They are never an officer's personal phone.

The website never asks for dues or payment, and never shares a WhatsApp group link. If anyone asks you to pay through a link claiming to be from the chapter, check with the executive first.
