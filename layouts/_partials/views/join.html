How to join

If you graduated from Imo State University and live in Ebonyi State, you are welcome in the chapter. Membership of GIMSUGA comes through a chapter.

  1. Talk to a member you know. New members are introduced to the chapter by an existing member.
  2. Don't know a member yet? Message or email the chapter below. An officer will talk with you and put you in touch.
  3. The executive takes it from there. They explain what membership involves and how to pay your dues, then send you a personal link to register on this site. See what registering looks like

This website never shows payment details and never takes payments. If anyone sends you a payment link claiming to be from the chapter, check with the executive first.

Contact the chapter

This reaches the chapter's own contact, looked after by a chapter office. The button starts a message for you.

Whichever way you get in touch, please include:

  • your name
  • the year you graduated, and your department
  • the town where you live
  • a member you know, if any
