    What else officers handle in this direction

    • Every direction: events, reports, the officer list after each election, and the record of the chapter's work.
    • Members area: members see, download and delete their own data themselves; officers handle the questions and complaints that remain, with the contracted developer. In this demo: 1 question waiting.
